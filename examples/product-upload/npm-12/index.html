Key features

Every circuit, measured from one hub

Circuit-level visibility

Up to 12 single-phase circuits, 4 three-phase circuits or a mix, each with its own power, energy and power-factor readings.

Power quality, not just kWh

  • Active, reactive and apparent power
  • Harmonics (THD) on voltage and current
  • Three-phase voltage unbalance

Installs without a shutdown

The hub clips onto a DIN rail and the sensors clip around live conductors. Our team installs and configures it for you.

Sized to any board

Split-core CTs from 100 A, or Rogowski coils up to 4,000 A for main incomers.

Alarms before trips

Overload, out-of-range and power-failure alerts go straight to the right people.

Built for the electrical room

Industrial-grade insulation and fast sampling give dependable readings in busy distribution boards.

No data gaps

Energy totals and min/max values stay on the hub for a year, and readings reach IoTRICs wirelessly.
